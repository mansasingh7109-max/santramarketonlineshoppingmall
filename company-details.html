5. About SANTRAJET MALL:

SANTRAJET MALL is a technology-driven hyperlocal e-commerce startup founded in 2024 from Pilibanga, Rajasthan. We started with a simple idea - "Why should people from small towns wait 5-7 days for Amazon/Flipkart delivery when local shops can deliver in 2 hours?"

Our platform allows customers to order Grocery, Fruits, Vegetables, Daily Needs, Cosmetics, Stationery, Electronics, Mobile Accessories, Fashion, and Home Decor items directly from their mobile. All orders are processed through WhatsApp for instant confirmation and real-time tracking. We have partnered with 25+ local vendors to provide 1000+ products with same-day delivery guarantee.
